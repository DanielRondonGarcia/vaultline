Custom destination direct placement: for native targets restoring to a custom destination that is not a filesystem root, the snapshot content of every configured source is extracted directly into the destination (no drive-letter or path components recreated under it), for one or several sources. When the destination is a POSIX root ("/"), a Windows drive root ("X:\\") or a UNC share root, the full recorded tree ("/<drive>/<path>/..." layout) is restored as-is. Docker behavior and original-route/same-path subtree restore remain unchanged.
